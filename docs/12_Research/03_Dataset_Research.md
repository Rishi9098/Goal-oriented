# 03 — Dataset Research

**Date:** 2026-07-08
**Status:** Research only.
**Evidence tiers:** [verified-web] / [training-knowledge] (cutoff Jan 2026) / [assumption]

**Framing note before any list:** per `04_Training_Strategy.md`, datasets serve **three distinct purposes** that must never be conflated: (1) **RAG corpus** (documents the model retrieves and cites at runtime — needs authority + effective-dating, not volume), (2) **fine-tuning data** (teaches format, tone, tool discipline — needs quality, not knowledge), (3) **evaluation data** (held-out ground truth — needs verified answers). A dataset excellent for one purpose is often wrong for another.

---

## 1. Government (India) — primary-source corpus material

These are **RAG corpus** sources, not training sets. They are the *only* acceptable authority tier for policy facts (consistent with Engineering Constitution Rule 4: never state an unverified financial fact).

| Source | Covers | License / terms | Quality | Update frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **incometaxindia.gov.in** — Income-tax Act, Rules, CBDT circulars/notifications, Finance Acts | Income Tax, Capital Gains, HUF taxation, deductions (80C/80D/80CCD…) | Indian statutes/notifications; Indian Copyright Act §52(1)(q) permits reproduction of Acts/rules **[training-knowledge — obtain legal confirmation before redistribution]** | Authoritative (Tier-0 source) | Annual (Finance Act) + rolling circulars |
| **data.gov.in** (Open Government Data Platform) | Scheme statistics, budget data, department datasets | **GODL-India** (open, attribution) [verified-web: [aigrants.in guide](https://aigrants.in/topics/how-to-fine-tune-a-model-using-indian-government-scheme-data-on-hugging-face)] | Variable per dataset | Variable |
| **myscheme.gov.in** | Central/state government schemes, eligibility descriptions | Government portal content [training-knowledge — terms to verify] | High for scheme discovery; not legally binding text | Rolling |
| **RBI** (master directions, FAQs), **SEBI** (regulations, investor education), **IRDAI** (insurance regulations, handbooks) | Investment, Insurance, market conduct | Regulator publications [training-knowledge — verify reuse terms per regulator] | Authoritative | Rolling |
| **PFRDA / npscra.nsdl.co.in** | NPS rules, tiers, withdrawal rules | Regulator/operator publications | Authoritative | Rolling |
| **EPFO** | EPF rules, interest rate notifications | Government body | Authoritative | Annual rate + circulars |
| **India Post / Ministry of Finance small-savings notifications** | PPF, SSY, SCSS rates and rules | Quarterly small-savings rate notifications | Authoritative — **this is exactly what Northstar's `scheme_rates` table already versions** | **Quarterly** (rates) |

**Key point:** Northstar already ingests the crown jewels. The `tax_sections`, `scheme_rates`, `scheme_eligibility_rules`, and `tax_slabs` tables are verified, versioned, effective-dated extracts of the sources above. **The RAG corpus should be built *around* these tables (explanatory prose, edge cases, procedures), never *duplicating* them** — duplication creates a second source of truth that can drift (violates the project's one-authority discipline).

## 5. Synthetic — **the most important category for Northstar, and the cheapest**

Northstar possesses something most teams must fake: **deterministic engines that produce verified ground truth with explanations attached.**

| Generator | What it yields | Ground truth |
|---|---|---|
| `run_simulation()` / `quick_probability()` over sampled goal/profile grids | "Explain this simulation result" training/eval pairs — inputs, probability, percentile bands | Exact (the engine's own output) |
| `optimizer.py` strategy outputs | "What are my options?" pairs with ranked strategies + trade-offs | Exact |
| `scheme_eligibility_service` over synthetic households | Eligibility narration pairs incl. *why* and *which rule fired*, in all three buckets | Exact (rules are versioned rows) |
| `compute_insurance_recommendation` over synthetic families | Recommendation narration with `what_information_was_used` / `what_is_missing` | Exact |
| Tool-call traces (question → correct tool + args → result → grounded answer) | **Tool-calling SFT data** — the single highest-value fine-tuning artifact | Exact by construction |
| Perturbed variants (wrong number injected into a draft answer) | **Groundedness-checker eval data** (catch the lie) | Exact |

Properties: unlimited volume, zero licensing risk, perfectly on-distribution (the assistant will face precisely these tasks), and ground truth guaranteed by the same engines that are already production-certified with 330 passing tests. Synthetic **user personas/goals** must be generated with realistic Indian income/expense distributions **[assumption to design carefully — sampling grids should be reviewed by a human for realism]**, and no real user data is ever used (see `08_Security.md` PII rules).

## 7. Explicit gaps and traps

1. **No public dataset covers Northstar's actual task** (narrating *this* product's engines). That is not a problem — it is the argument for synthetic-from-engines (§5).
2. **License traps identified:** Financial PhraseBank (non-commercial); GPT-distilled instruction sets (provider-terms taint); Gemma-3-era and Llama-generated synthetic data carry their own output-use clauses [training-knowledge — legal review before using *any* model-generated dataset produced by a third party].
3. **Estate planning and HUF *procedures*** (not HUF taxation) have no reliable open corpus — mark as out-of-scope topics the assistant must decline gracefully, consistent with Product Principle #8 (unverified stays visibly unverified).
4. **Update-frequency obligation:** small-savings rates change **quarterly**; tax slabs **annually**; circulars **continuously**. Whatever enters the RAG corpus inherits Northstar's existing versioning duty (Engineering Constitution Rule 2). A corpus document without an effective-date is a defect, not a document (enforced in `05_RAG_Architecture.md` metadata schema).
